Every week, someone asks me a legal question over a cup of tea. A friend about to sign a rent agreement. A cousin whose cheque was returned. A neighbour wondering whether a handwritten will is "valid".

The questions are almost always good ones, and the answers are almost always simpler than people fear. But the answers are hard to find, buried in bare acts, long judgments and websites written by lawyers for other lawyers.

## What this blog will do

Each article will take **one real question** and answer it in plain language:

1. **What the law says**, with the actual section, so you can check it yourself.
2. **What it means in practice**: the deadlines, the documents, the usual pitfalls.
3. **What you can do next**, including when it's time to speak to an advocate.

## What it won't do

It won't give advice on your specific case. Law depends on facts, and two situations that look identical often aren't. Treat these articles as a map, not a destination.

If there's a question you'd like answered, [write to me](/contact/). I read every message.
