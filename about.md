Hello, I'm **{{ site.author_name }}**.

I'm an advocate based in {{ site.location }}. Over the years I've noticed that most of the people who come to me didn't need to be in trouble. They signed something they didn't understand, missed a deadline they didn't know existed, or waited too long because the law felt intimidating.

This blog is my attempt to fix a small part of that. Each article takes one real question and answers it in plain language: what the law says, what it means in practice, and what you can do next.

## What you'll find here

- **Plain explanations.** No Latin, no jargon, or where jargon is unavoidable, an explanation of it.
- **Practical steps.** Checklists, timelines and the documents you'll need.
- **Honest limits.** When a situation really needs a lawyer, I'll say so.
